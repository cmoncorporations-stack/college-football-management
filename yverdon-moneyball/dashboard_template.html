Method

  • Next matches (moneyball/matchprep.py). A data-driven approach in the spirit of clubs that made it their method, such as AC Como; Como's internal model is not public and none of its parameters is reused. (1) Shot-based xG: Transfermarkt publishes total shots, shots off target and shots saved but no expected goals; goals ≈ a × shots on target + b × shots off target, fitted on the 2026/27 Challenge League matches. This is not a location-based xG. (2) Team ratings: Poisson model with attack, defence and home advantage on the 2026/27 Challenge League, target = 50 % goals + 50 % shot xG, shrunk towards the 2025/26 table (worth 6 matches). (3) Cup: Poisson regression on the 2026/27 Swiss Cup first round between professional clubs and clubs from a division with a known coefficient (log λ = α + β × ln(coefficient ratio) + home), then the opponent is placed within its own league; extra time at one third of the expected goals, penalties at 50 %. (4) XI: available players, positions actually played this season, the current coach's 4-3-3; selection score = 0.45 Sport + 0.35 share of minutes over the last 3 matches + 0.20 26/27 dynamics. (5) Scorers: P(score) = 1 − e−λ × share, share = goals per 90 shrunk towards the position × expected minutes. (6) Projection: Monte Carlo of the fixtures scheduled up to the winter break.
  • Sport (Transfermarkt only, last two league seasons): each line is scored within its sub-position as a percentile rank in the pool (players with 900+ minutes), after shrinkage towards the sub-position median when the sample is short (confidence = minutes / (minutes + 900)).
    • Midfielders, forwards: output (goals + 0.7 × assists) per 90 in Challenge League equivalent (× level) → percentile; 45 % (midfielders) or 60 % (forwards) of the score, the rest availability (minutes per season / 2,400) and discipline (10 %).
    • Goalkeepers: never any attacking output. Defensive index = clean-sheet rate + a goals-conceded term: for a keeper sharing the job (under 85 % of the team's minutes), 1 − (his goals conceded per 90 ÷ the team's), capped at ± 0.5; for a sole starter (ratio of 1 by construction), the team's league rank, from −0.3 (last) to +0.3 (first). Percentile among goalkeepers only → 65 %; availability capped at 25 %; discipline 10 %. Without goals-conceded data: goalkeeper median and "low" confidence.
    • Centre-backs: team defensive results — team goals conceded per match against the league median (0.5 = median; 1 = half as many; 0 = twice as many) → percentile among centre-backs → 55 %; availability 30 %; cards as a negative (discipline 15 %). Transfermarkt has no individual defensive data: confidence capped at 50 % and shown as "low".
    • Full-backs: 50 % defensive profile (same formula as centre-backs) + 50 % attacking output (goals + 0.7 × assists, percentile among full-backs) → 55 %; availability 35 %; discipline 10 %.
    Everything is multiplied by (0.55 + 0.45 × level), level = league coefficient / 1.45 (Super League ≈ 1, bounded 0.3-1.2), and by (1 − injury days / 180, at most 0.5), then divided by 0.85 and capped at 100.
  • 2026/27 season dynamics (Transfermarkt match-by-match log, all competitions, as of ). Before the league level, the raw sub-position score is blended with a "dynamics" score: weight = × confidence, confidence = team matches / (matches + ) — at 10 matches dynamics weighs about 16 % of the score, rising as the season goes on. Dynamics score (0-1) = 0.30 start share (starts / matches in which the player was available, injuries and suspensions excluded) + 0.20 share of the team's minutes + 0.15 role trend (share of minutes over the team's last 5 matches minus the first 5, re-centred) + 0.15 form (output over the last 5 matches played / season output, 1.0 = his average; goalkeepers and centre-backs: goals conceded on the pitch, inverted) + 0.10 big-game factor (output against the top half of the league table on the log date / bottom half) + 0.05 availability (1 − matches missed through injury or suspension / matches) + 0.05 consistency (1 − standard deviation of minutes per match played / 45). An indicator that cannot be computed counts as 0.5. A player without a log keeps his score and gets the "26/27 season not read" tag.
    • All-competition minutes. Current-season aggregates now count the league (weight 1.0, at its coefficient), the national cup (weight , at the level of the player's league) and European cups (weight 1.0, level by default, opponent league unknown); the current season is weighted min(1, team matches / ) against 2025/26, so it counts more and more as matchdays pass. Displayed minutes remain actual minutes.
    • Tags. "In form" (form ≥ 1.3 and ≥ 3 matches played), "Losing his place" (trend ≤ −0.3), "Winning his place" (≥ +0.3), "Established starter" (starts ≥ 80 % and ≥ 6 matches), "Breakout 26/27" (output per 90 ≥ 1.5 × 2025/26 with ≥ 450 minutes; goalkeepers: goals conceded per 90 divided by 1.5), "Currently injured" (latest status injured), "Small sample" (under 900 actual minutes).
    • Log statuses. A player's "detailed stats" page is rendered client-side by Transfermarkt (tmapi API not reachable): the log is rebuilt from club fixture lists, match reports (goals, assists, substitutions, cards, minute read from the clock sprite) and line-ups (XI, bench, position played). Absences: injured (player injury history, club's current absences), suspended (red card in the previous match or suspension listed by the club), otherwise not in squad; matches before the player joined the club do not count. A match without a published line-up is ignored.
  • League coefficients. A player's level is the minute-weighted average of his leagues' coefficients over his last two seasons with minutes, keeping only competitions with a known coefficient (cups and "Total" rows ignored; 0.8 if none is known). Reference Challenge League = 1.00. Exact Transfermarkt codes: . Lower divisions, by code prefix: . Worked example: 1,080 minutes in the Challenge League (1.00) and 900 minutes in the 1st League Classic (0.45) → (1,080 × 1.00 + 900 × 0.45) / 1,980 = 0.75.
  • Value: 40 % undervaluation (residual of a regression log(market value) ~ sport score + age + age² + league + position across the whole pool, so that "plays in the National" is not mistaken for "undervalued"), 25 % contract leverage (≤ 6 months = free or pre-contract), 15 % 12-month value trend, 20 % expected net gain: projected value in 24 months (age curve per line, peaking at 26 for midfielders and forwards, 27 for defenders, 29 for goalkeepers, modulated by the last 12 months' momentum) minus the estimated fee, relative to the current value. A player without a Transfermarkt value gets the value predicted by this regression, marked (*): his Value score weighs half as much in the final score and he is excluded from the automatic transfer plan.
  • Fan fit: regional anchoring (normalised birthplace — Genf, Genève and Geneva are the same city — linked to its municipality, canton and zone: North Vaud catchment 1.00, Vaud 0.85, French-speaking Switzerland 0.70, Switzerland 0.45, cross-border 0.40, international 0.10; a Yverdon youth club counts as the catchment, a French-speaking Swiss youth club as French-speaking Switzerland, and the better of the two readings is kept for everyone), French language, loyalty (average time per club), media potential (social media + attacking flair) and link with the club, weighted by the fan DNA above.
  • Final score = weighted average of the three scores × (0.85 + 0.30 × positional need).
  • Estimated fee = 0 within six months of the contract's end, otherwise 35 to 100 % of market value depending on the time left. An order of magnitude, not an offer.

Sources

  • Transfermarkt (direct reading of www.transfermarkt.com pages): profiles, statistics for the last two seasons (each club's "Squad statistics" page: league, and for the current season every competition in the selector), goalkeepers' goals conceded and clean sheets (each competition's "Clean sheets" page, detailed view), league tables (goals conceded and rank of each team), value history, transfers, and for 2026/27 the match-by-match log (club fixtures, match reports with shot statistics, line-ups, absences, injury history). Values in euros.
  • Yverdon Sport Fanbase Manager cockpit: penetration index, CMON_SCORE categories, Brevo campaigns, Metricool audiences.

Limitations

  • Next matches: no location-based xG (Sofascore and Opta not reachable), shot-based xG only; the model ignores fatigue, weather and the opponent's tactics; the 4-3-3 rests on a single match under the new coach; team ratings stay fixed during the simulation; Old Boys' level is inferred from 9 professional-versus-amateur cup matches.
  • Planned sources not active on this pool: Sofascore (xG, xA, rating; returns 403 outside a browser) and API-Football (rating, shots, key passes; no key). Without them the Sport score relies on Transfermarkt goals, assists, minutes, discipline, goals conceded and team results.
  • 2026/27 log: Transfermarkt does not publish suspensions match by match; they are inferred (red card in the previous match, suspension listed by the club). A player's matches with another club before he joined are not read. The level of European opponents is unknown in the aggregates (1.3 by default).
  • Transfermarkt has no individual defensive statistics (duels, interceptions): centre-backs are scored on their team's results, hence "low" confidence. A sole-starter goalkeeper is scored on his team's league position, not on his saves.
  • Transfermarkt does not give players' follower counts: media potential relies on having an account, not on its audience.
  • The club CRM does not hold fans' ages (date of birth filled in for 36 %): generational fit is not modelled.
  • Scope: men's team only.
  • A decision-support tool: it sorts a pool, it does not replace video or live scouting.
